Guidance Notes

Completion of the staff performance management form

Use this annual form to agree performance expectations, review progress, assess results and identify development needs.

  1. Performance planning: Agree key result areas, specific targets and the resources needed with the appraisee.
  2. Progress review: Meet during the year to discuss progress, record remarks and agree any needed adjustments.
  3. Review and appraisal: Assess end-of-year results and the relevant core and non-core competencies.
  4. Decision making: Discuss recognition, development, training and next steps arising from the appraisal.

Performance planning

Targets should be clear, measurable, realistic and time-framed, and should reflect the employee’s responsibilities and the organization’s objectives.
